# SPEC.md — Project Specification

> **Status**: `FINALIZED`

## Vision
StartupBrew is a specialized auditing service for early-stage startups that goes beyond code and metrics. Using a proprietary framework, StartupBrew stress-tests both the startup's operational robustness and its founders' resilience under pressure. The goal is to provide a clear roadmap and viability report before startups burn investor cash or hit critical failure points.

## Goals
1. **Initial Trust Building**: Create a professional, high-converting landing page to establish credibility since the brand is currently unknown.
2. **Lead Generation**: Capture interest from newly launched startups through a compelling Call to Action ("Connect with us" / "Apply for a Free Audit").
3. **Process Obfuscation**: Pitch the "secret framework" in an engaging, phased manner without revealing the actual proprietary steps (e.g., Phase 1: Founder Resilience Check, Phase 2: System Stress Test, Phase 3: Viability Roadmap).
4. **Acquire Early Adopters**: Facilitate the strategy of offering free initial audits (via cold outreach) to gather the first 4 testimonial proofs before monetizing the service.

## Non-Goals (Out of Scope)
- Building the actual auditing dashboard or automated testing platform.
- Creating the detailed questionnaire or report generation system.
- E-commerce or payment gateways (monetization happens post-testimonials).
- Complex user authentication or portal access.

## Users
Newly launched startup founders (primarily in India) who are ambitious and willing to subject themselves and their startups to rigorous stress-testing to prove their viability and robustness.

## Constraints
- **Technical**: Must be a fast, responsive, and visually appealing static landing page.
- **Content**: The exact auditing framework must remain secret; copy must pitch the *value* of the phases without the *details*.
- **Timeline**: Needs to be deployed quickly to support the immediate cold-outreach campaign (email/IG DMs).

## Success Criteria
- [ ] Landing page is live and visibly professional/premium.
- [ ] CTA effectively captures lead information (e.g., a simple contact form).
- [ ] Phased process is well-communicated.
- [ ] Page load speed and mobile responsiveness are optimized.
